## 1. PANAMA — Fundación Privada (Ley 25/1995, Texto Refundido)
**Primary source:** [vLex Texto Refundido, Ley 25/1995](https://www.vlex.com.mx/vid/ley-no-25-1995-4753628.vlex) (saved: `raw/panama_ley25_vlex.txt`); fees: [panamadigital.gob.pa](https://panamadigital.gob.pa/)

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Founders | ≥ 1 natural/juridical person; **no nationality or residency restriction** on founders | Art. 1 |
| Purpose | Non-profit; incidental commercial activity allowed | Art. 3 |
| Form | Private deed **with notary-authenticated signatures** suffices (no full public deed) | Art. 4 |
| Endowment (patrimonio inicial) | **≥ B/. 10,000** | Art. 5 |
| Name | Must include the word "fundación" | Art. 5 |
| Council | **No fewer than 3 members** (unless a single persona jurídica); full designation incl. members' addresses in the act | Arts. 5, 17 |
| Domicilio | Required in Panama | Art. 5 |
| **Residency obligation** | **ONLY ONE in the entire law:** *agente residente* — a Panamanian lawyer/law firm residing in Panama, who refrends the fundación act before registration. Grep of whole statute for "resid" = exactly 1 hit (Art. 5). No residency duty on founders, council members, or officers. | Art. 5 |
| Registration | Registro Público inscription; confidential registry — disclosure punished (6 months prison, B/. 50,000) | Art. 35 |
| Filing fees | B/. 0.60 per B/. 1,000 of capital (up to B/. 1,000,000); B/. 0.12/mil above; B/. 20 per no-par action; processing 1–2 weeks | panamadigital.gob.pa |

**Verdict: SETTLED.** The only residency hook is the *agente residente* (Art. 5) — a lawyer of record, not a resident director/founder.

## 2. ARGENTINA — Fundación / Asociación civil (Ley 19.836, national)
**Primary source:** [Infoleg, Ley 19.836](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/verNorma.do?id=94751) (saved: `raw/arg_ley19836.txt`); supplementary: [RG IGJ 6/2004 (naming/domicile)](https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/verNorma.do?id=94787) (saved as `raw/arg_rg15_2024.txt` — **file is mislabeled; content is RG 6/2004, not "RG 15/2024"**)

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Nature | Bien común, sin fines de lucro; ≥ 1 founding person; authorization per Art. 45 Código Civil (judicial authorization, not mere filing) | Art. 1 |
| Endowment | **NO fixed minimum.** Art. 2: endowment must only "posibilite razonablemente el cumplimiento de los fines"; future committed aportes count | Art. 2 |
| Form | Public instrument **OR private instrument with signatures certified by an escribano** | Art. 3 |
| Founder data | Naturales: name, age, marital status, nationality, profession, domicile, DNI — **no foreigner restriction** | Art. 3 |
| Endowment in cash | Deposited in an official bank during the trámite; non-cash needs inventory + valuation | Art. 4 |
| Council | **Minimum 3 members** | Art. 10 |
| Council composition | May include **public institutions and private non-profit legal entities** | Art. 12 |
| Residency/nationality of council | **None** | — |
| Naming/domicile (CABA) | Name core must state principal objective(s); must be in Spanish (foreign additions allowed); sede identified to street level in CABA | RG IGJ 6/2004, arts. 1–2 |
| Inspection | Prior inspection visit mandatory in authorization proceedings (exceptions for foundations per Art. 2(2) of Ley 19.836) | RG IGJ 6/2004, art. 6 |

**Verdict: SETTLED (national level).** No fixed endowment floor, no nationality/residency requirement. Note: provincial foundations (outside CABA) follow analogous provincial law; national authorization runs through IGJ CABA.

## 3. CHILE — Corporaciones y Fundaciones (CC Título XXXIII, arts. 545–564, as amended by Ley 20.500/2011)
**Primary sources:** [BCN, Ley 20.500 (amendment text)](https://www.icnl.org/wp-content/uploads/Chile_chilena.pdf) (official BCN reprint, saved: `raw/chile_ley20500_icnl.txt`); [BCN, Corporaciones y fundaciones summary](https://obtienearchivo.bcn.cl/obtienearchivo?id=repositorio/10221/20193/5/Corporaciones_y_fundaciones.pdf); local mirror: [DerechoPedia CC](https://www.derechopedia.com/legislacion/chile/civil/codigo-civil/titulo-33/) (saved: `raw/chile_cc_title33_full.txt`)

**Data-quality flag CLEARED:** the mirror's art. 545 wording ("fundaciones de beneficencia pública") was suspected to be pre-20.500. The official BCN text of Ley 20.500 art. 38.1º shows the amendment **kept** that phrase and only added the association/foundation definitions — the mirror is the correct post-20.500 text. No correction needed.

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Legal personality | From **inscription** in Registro Nacional de Personas Jurídicas sin Fines de Lucro (Registro Civil) | Art. 548 |
| Constitutive act | **Public OR private instrument before notario, oficial del Registro Civil, or authorized municipal official**; copy deposited at secretaría municipal within 30 days; municipal secretary may object for 30 days (objection period lapses silently) | Art. 548 |
| Act contents | Identifies parties, expresses will to constitute, approves statutes, designates initial authorities | Art. 548-1 |
| Statutory minimums | Statutes must state "Los bienes que forman su patrimonio inicial, **si los hubiere**, y la forma en que se aporten" → **NO fixed endowment minimum** | Art. 548-2(d) |
| Foundation statutes add | Assets/rights contributed by founder + rules for applying resources to fundacional fines and determining beneficiaries | Art. 548-2 |
| Name | Must reflect nature/purpose; no confusion with existing entities (20-yr rule for natural persons) | Art. 548-3 |
| Board (directorio) | **≥ 3 members**, mandate ≤ 5 yrs; persons convicted of aflictiva penalty ineligible; reemplazante fills vacancies; president represents; simple-majority quorum; tie broken by president | Art. 551 |
| Directors' pay | Unpaid (expenses reimbursable); solidary liability up to culpa leve | Arts. 551-1, 551-2 |
| Property & surplus | Corporations and foundations may acquire/hold/dispose of any property; **no distribution of rentas/utilidades/excedentes among associates, even on dissolution** | Art. 556 |
| Oversight | **Ministerio de Justicia** fiscalización (may demand minutes, accounts, books; non-compliance = infracción grave); GAAP accounting, annual memoria + balance, external audit above Minister-set thresholds; related economic activities permitted, revenues only to purposes/patrimony; minutes book + permanent registers mandatory | Arts. 557, 557-1, 557-2, 557-3 |
| Statute modification (foundation) | **Only by directorio agreement + favorable Ministerio de Justicia informe**, and only if "conveniente al interés fundacional"; founder may prohibit | Art. 558 |
| Dissolution | Term expiry, EGA (2/3 of attendees for associations), final judgment on Ministerio petition, statutes; assets per statutes else to the State | Arts. 559, 561 |
| Nationality/residency of founders/board | **NONE** — grep of arts. 545–569 for "resid" = 2 hits, both "reemplazante"; "extranjero"/"nacionalidad"/"chilen" = 0 | — |

**Verdict: SETTLED.** Foundation = single-founder vehicle, no endowment floor, board ≥ 3, state oversight by Ministerio de Justicia, no nationality/residency duties.

## 4. NEW ZEALAND — Incorporated Society (Incorporated Societies Act 2022, Public Act 2022 No 12, ver. 27 Nov 2025)
> **⚠ TARGET CONFIRMATION NEEDED:** NZ was added as a **working assumption** on the user's target list. Confirm this is the intended vehicle type (Incorporated Society under the 2022 Act — a non-financial-gain vehicle, NOT a company/charity trust).

**Primary sources:** [legislation.govt.nz, ISA 2022 PDF](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2022/12/latest/DLM6495727.html) (saved: `raw/nz_isa2022.txt`); [Incorporated Societies Register, Companies Office](https://is-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/) (fee)

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Minimum members** | **10 or more persons** — s 8(1) verbatim: *"Any 10 or more persons may apply to incorporate a society under this Act for any lawful purpose other than a purpose of being carried on for the financial gain of any of its members."* **This corrects the earlier working assumption of a 3-member floor.** A **body corporate counts as 3 members** for this test (also s 74) | s 8(1), s 14 |
| Purpose | **Not carried on for financial gain of members** (offence, fine ≤ $50,000); "financial gain" = distributions, shares/stock, disposable member interests | ss 22–23 |
| Constitution | **Mandatory.** Must contain: name; purposes; membership admission (incl. consent, s 76); cessation; register-of-members arrangements; committee composition, size, powers, meeting/quorum/voting procedure, officer election & removal; contact-person appointment (s 113); financial management controls; and no member rights/interests in property | ss 25–27 |
| Officers | **Must be natural persons** (under-16, undischarged bankrupt etc. disqualified) | s 47(1) |
| **Residency obligation** | **ONLY ONE:** ≥ 1 (max 3) **contact person** at all times, who must be **≥ 18 and ordinarily resident in NZ**; vacancy must be filled within **20 working days** | ss 113–115 |
| Application contents | Prescribed info; proposed name; ≥ 1 contact person name + contact details; copy of proposed constitution; for every officer: info + consent + certificate of not being disqualified; fee; prescribed manner | s 9 |
| Incorporation fee | **$88.89 + GST** (regulatory; not in Act text — Act contains only infringement fees ≤ $1,000 and court fines ≤ $3,000) | is-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz |
| Registration | Incorporated by filing with the Registrar of Companies (Companies Office) | s 9(g) |

**Verdict: SETTLED.** Key structural notes for comparison: the 10-person floor is a genuine constraint (vs. 1 founder in Panama/Chile, 1 in Argentina); a company can substitute 3 of the 10 slots via s 14; the sole NZ residency hook is the contact person (not directors, not members).
