# c1 research notes (verified sources only; to be condensed into final JSON)
Order: 0 Canada, 1 United States, 2 Cuba, 3 Jamaica, 4 Bahamas, 5 Barbados, 6 Trinidad & Tobago,
7 Antigua & Barbuda, 8 Grenada, 9 St. Kitts & Nevis, 10 St. Lucia, 11 St. Vincent & Grenadines,
12 Dominica, 13 Dominican Republic, 14 Haiti, 15 Mexico, 16 Guatemala, 17 Belize, 18 El Salvador, 19 Honduras

0 Canada: SEPARATE CRA application (Form T2050) after incorporation, under Income Tax Act;
qualifying charitable purposes; designated charitable organization/public/private foundation.
Benefits: charity exempt from income tax; issues official donation receipts (donor deduction).
Time: CRA service standard ~9 months from complete application (commonly 6-9 months).

1 United States: SEPARATE IRS application Form 1023 (or 1023-EZ for small orgs). Criteria:
organized & operated exclusively for charitable/religious/educational/scientific purposes, no
private inurement, no substantial lobbying. Benefits: federal income tax exemption, donor
deduction, most states mirror 501(c)(3) for state income tax. Time: not officially published;
commonly ~6-12 months.

2 Cuba: No separate exemption application. Art. 101 of tax law (Gaceta Oficial 053/2012):
exempt from income tax (Impuesto sobre Utilidades): legally recognized religious/fraternal
institutions in the Registro de Asociaciones; international non-profit institutions with legal
personality. No donor-deduction regime. Category-based, automatic upon legal recognition.

3 Jamaica: Registration with Charities Authority (DCFS is designate authority) under Charities
Act 2013 + 2022 Regulations, separate from Companies Office; gives charitable status cert.
Tax: Income Tax Act s.13(1)(q) - Minister of Finance approves institution by gazette order ->
donor deduction (donations capped at 1/20 of statutory income). TJA exemption list also exempts
income of corporations/associations operating exclusively for religious/charitable/scientific/
educational purposes. Approval time unpublished.

4 Bahamas: NPO incorporated via RGD under Companies Act Ch.308 s.14 (charitable objects);
NPO Act defines NPOs. Tax: NO separate published charitable tax-exempt approval found -
charitable status not automatically tax-exempt; no donor-deduction scheme identified.

5 Barbados: Charity registration at CAIPO (Charities regime, s.5(6)(a)) unless exempt; exempt
charities listed in First Schedule (exemption list). Income Tax Act Cap.73; s.10 amended by
Income Tax (Amendment and Validation) Act 2024 (charitable provisions). Benefits: exemption for
listed/exempt charities; donor deduction under Cap.73 charitable provisions. Time unpublished.

6 Trinidad & Tobago: "Approved charity" status (Ministry of Finance); donations under deeds of
covenant to approved charities deductible by donors up to 15% of total income (PwC Tax
Summaries). Charitable organizations exempt from corporate income tax (Income Tax Act Ch.79:04).
Time unpublished.

7 Antigua & Barbuda: Automatic category exemption: Income Tax Act Cap.212 s.8(d) - income of
any ecclesiastical, charitable, or educational institution of a public character, insofar as not
derived from trade/business. No separate tax-exempt application. Donor deduction: no scheme
identified.

8 Grenada: Confirmed from Cap.149 text: s.25(1)(q) - income of religious/charitable/educational
institution of public character exempt from income tax (not from profit business). Donor
deduction: expenditure by way of donation to charitable org / non-profit company / professional
institute APPROVED BY THE COMPTROLLER (GRA), if not engaged in business for profit. Automatic
by category + Comptroller approval for deduction.

9 St. Kitts & Nevis: Ch.20.22 Income Tax Act s.7(1) lists exemptions (local authority, trade
union, friendly society, ...). Charitable institution exemption + donor deduction not confirmed
in accessible text (official PDF AES-encrypted). NEEDS ONE MORE CHECK.

10 St. Lucia: IRD FAQ: donation claim allowed only under DEED OF COVENANT, period >=3 years,
amount allowable restricted to twenty-five per cent [of assessable income?]. Charitable status
via Inland Revenue. NEEDS CONFIRMATION of % and org exemption.

11 St. Vincent & Grenadines: Confirmed Cap.435: exemption s.44(o) (income of religious,
charitable, educational institution of public character, not from profit business). s.58:
deduction for deed of covenant to approved religious/charitable/educational institution/fund of
public character, Comptroller satisfied, deed >=5 years, deduction limited to 5% of assessable
income.

12 Dominica: Income Tax Act ch.67:01 s.25 = income exemptions (incl. charitable institutions).
IRD FAQ: donations only allowed if institution listed on APPROVED LIST BY CABINET, last update
S.R.O 47 of 2007. Company incorporation itself gives no tax status.

13 Dominican Republic: Legal registration at PGR (Ley 122-05) then SEPARATE DGII exemption
application (ASFL regime). DGII requirements (dgii.gov.do ASFL page + Revista ASFL PDF):
deposit of legal base, RNC, etc. Benefits: ISR + ITBIS exemption; donor deduction for
donations (Ley 112-05 Art. 35: up to 15% of taxable income). Time unpublished.

14 Haiti: ONG registered under decree 14 Sept 1989 (MPCE). Tax: DGI; ONG can be excluded from
IS (corporate income tax) - NOT YET VERIFIED in source. NEEDS ONE MORE CHECK.

15 Mexico: SEPARATE SAT "donataria autorizada" authorization (Ficha 19/ISR, Anexo 2). SAT max
3 months to resolve. Benefits: donor deduction 50% of donation up to 15% of taxable income
(LISR Art. 23); org's income used for authorized exempt activities is ISR-exempt. Registration
with Public Registry (CFC) gives no tax status.

16 Guatemala: Income Tax Law (Ley del Impuesto Sobre la Renta): NPO eligible for income tax
exemption if objectives charitable/assistance/social service/culture/scientific research/
education/arts/sports (Cof.org country note). Donation deduction: LISR Art.28 - donations to
funds/foundations/associations deductible (up to 25% of total income, cap 10% - to confirm).
MINGOB "reconocimiento" for fundacion separate from legal registration.

17 Belize: Automatic category exemption, Income and Business Tax Act Cap.55: earnings of
ecclesiastical, charitable, educational institutions exempt. Donor: charitable contributions
relief for individuals capped BZ$250. No separate application.

18 El Salvador: Registration (Min. Interior, Registro de Asociaciones y Fundaciones) does NOT
automatically confer exemption. Must be CALIFICADA by DGII (Min. Hacienda) as excluded from
ISR obligation (LAFSFL Art.7: "declaradas de utilidad pública previa calificación de la DGI").
Donor deduction: ISR Law Art.32(4) donations to entities under Art.6 (public utility).
NOTE: May 2025 Ley de Agentes Extranjeros: 30% levy on foreign-sourced funds of NGOs.

19 Honduras: ONGD law (Ley Especial de Fomento para ONGD), DIRRS/Secretaría de Justicia. Tax:
donation deduction under Ley del ISR - NEEDS ONE MORE CHECK.
