# Legal-entity research: registered associations in North Africa
**Libya · Algeria · Tunisia · Morocco · Mauritania** — consolidated report, statutory text verified against primary sources. Emphasis: annual reporting, mandatory financial audit (commissaire aux comptes / CAC), audit thresholds, dissolution / de-registration. All web content treated as untrusted; citations below.

---

## 1. LIBYA — NGO (Law No. 19 of 2001 on the reorganisation of NGOs, issued 28/12/2001)
**Primary source:** [DCAF Libya Legal Database, Law No. (19) of 2001 (English)](https://security-legislation.ly/latest-laws/law-no-19-of-2001-on-the-reorganization-of-ngos/) (fetched, saved `/tmp/ly19.txt`); Arabic: [lawsociety.ly](https://lawsociety.ly/legislation/). Caveat: HRW (2023) and the 2013 GNA circulars are cited by the task scope; post-2011 legal limbo (2011 draft NGO law declared Law 19 void) means practice varies by region.

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Constitution | ≥ **50 founding members** sign by-laws; legal personality by **promulgation decree** (Secretariat of the General People's Committee, national level, or district People's Committee) entered in a register; 50 LYD fee | Arts. 2, 6, 7 |
| Governance | Congress + People's Committee (elected by congress) | Arts. 20–21, 28–29 |
| **Annual compliance** | Annual **budget, final accounts, People's Committee reports and account-auditing reports** shown to members **≥ 2 weeks before the annual congress**, which convenes **within 3 months of fiscal-year end** to ratify them | **Arts. 11, 21** |
| **Audit (CAC)** | **No CAC / statutory-auditor regime.** The "account auditing report" required for the congress is an internal/by-laws-level requirement; no monetary audit threshold exists in the law | Art. 11 |
| Bank | Cash deposited in a bank designated by the People's Committee, in the organisation's name | Art. 12 |
| Foreign relations | **Prohibited** to join foreign organisations or **accept donations/gifts from foreign entities** without prior approval of the Secretariat; donation collection only within purpose and with permit | Arts. 14, 15 |
| Oversight | Secretariat may suspend a violating resolution and must sue for annulment within 30 days; annulment by first-instance court ruling | Arts. 30–31 |
| **Dissolution** | **Administrative (no court):** reasoned decree where grave violation of law/public order/decency, repeated by-laws violations, proven inability to fulfil purpose, misuse of funds, or **congress fails to convene for two consecutive years**. HQ closure ≤ 3 months precedes. Liquidation committee appointed by the decree; residual funds per by-laws, à défaut to the **social solidarity fund**; by-laws may not devolve assets to members. Responsible committee members barred from any other organisation for **5 years** | Arts. 4, 35–36, 38–40 |
| Penalties | False statements/records: ≤ 3 months' imprisonment and/or ≤ 500 LYD; other violations: **fine ≥ 500 LYD**, doubled for the Secretariat/Committee on negligence | Arts. 41–42 |

**Verdict: SETTLED.** No annual filing to a state authority per se (the ratification is internal at the annual congress); no CAC at any threshold; dissolution is purely administrative by decree, not judicial.

## 2. ALGERIA — Association (Law n° 12-06 du 12/01/2012, as amended 2020)
**Primary source:** official JORAD text (extraction saved `/tmp/d1206.txt`, arts. 19–21, 33–43 verified line-by-line).

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Registration | Declaration/registration with wilaya (province) authorities | law, title I |
| **Annual compliance** | **Universal:** annual **moral and financial report + PV** of the GA submitted to the competent public authority **within 30 days of the GA's adoption** | **Art. 19** |
| Documentation duty | Refusal to furnish documents: **fine 2,000–5,000 DA** | Art. 20 |
| Insurance | Liability insurance required | Art. 21 |
| Finance | All resources booked in the budget income account; **single bank account**; public grants only for *intérêt général / utilité publique* associations per *cahier des charges*, subordinated to a *contrat programme*; subsidy misuse → suspension or definitive withdrawal | Arts. 33–35, 37 |
| **Audit (CAC)** | **Universal, NO monetary threshold:** double-entry bookkeeping **validated by a CAC** (commissaire aux comptes) | **Art. 38** |
| Suspensions | Sovereignty/internal-affairs offences → suspension or dissolution; violations of arts. 15, 18, 19, 28, 30, 55, 60, 63 → **suspension ≤ 6 months**; suspension preceded by **mise en demeure** — if ineffective after **3 months**, administrative suspension decision, appeal before the administrative court | Arts. 39–41 |
| **Dissolution** | **Voluntary** (by members per by-laws) **or judicial**, notified to the approving authority; for *intérêt général / utilité publique* associations the authority, previously informed, ensures continuity of activity. The authority may also petition the administrative court for dissolution (out-of-scope activities, foreign funds in violation) | Arts. 42–43 |

**Verdict: SETTLED.** The strictest of the five: CAC-validated double-entry bookkeeping and the 30-day annual reporting duty are **universal** (all registered associations), with no resource threshold.

## 3. TUNISIA — Association (Décret-loi n° 2011-88 du 24/09/2011)
**Primary source:** [ACM, décret-loi 2011-88 (PDF)](https://www.acm.gov.tn/upload/1410083987.pdf) (fetched, saved `/tmp/tl88.txt`, all 49 articles); JoRT: [pist.tn](https://www.pist.tn/jort/2011/2011F/Jo0742011.pdf).

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Constitution | Declaration at prefecture (no prior authorisation) | law, ch. I |
| **Audit (CAC) — tiered thresholds** | Resources **> 100,000 TND** → CAC (member of the OECT list, *or* "techniciens en comptabilité"); **> 1,000,000 TND** → **CAC from the OECT list only**. Appointed by the GA; **3-year non-renewable mandate**; audit performed per OECT norms; CAC report to the **Secrétaire Général du Gouvernement (SGG)** + board chair **within 1 month** of presentation of the financial statements | **Art. 43** |
| **Publication** | Financial statements + audit report **published** (written medium / website) **within 1 month of approval** | Art. 43 |
| Public funds | Associations benefiting from public funding → **annual report to the Cour des comptes** | Art. 44 |
| Records | Kept **10 years** | Art. 42 |
| Statutory dissolution clauses | By-laws **must** designate the organ empowered to decide dissolution/merger/split AND "impérativement" fix the modalities of provisional suspension or dissolution + liquidation rules for voluntary dissolution (ch. II) | ch. II |
| **Dissolution — sanctions cascade** | Violations of arts. 3, 4, 8(2), 9, 10(2), 16–19, 27, 33(2,4), 35, 37(1), 38(1), 39(1), 40(4), 41–44 → **(1) mise en demeure** by the SGG, cure ≤ **30 days**; **(2) suspension d'activité** (≤ **30 days**) by *ordonnance sur requête* of the président du tribunal de première instance de Tunis, référé appeal; **(3) dissolution** by **judgment of the tribunal de première instance de Tunis** at the request of the SGG **or any person with an interest**, only once the infraction persists despite mise en demeure + suspension + exhaustion of remedies. Dissolution & liquidation governed by the Code des procédures civiles et commerciales. **No "radiation" mechanism** in the law | **Art. 45** |
| Transitions | Abrogates loi 59-154 (07/11/1959) and LO 93-80 (26/07/1993); existing associations: 1-year compliance window for ch. II constitution rules | Arts. 46–48 |

**Verdict: SETTLED.** The only one of the five with **monetary audit thresholds** (100k / 1M TND). No standalone dissolution chapter — the operative mechanism is the Art. 45 three-tier cascade ending in **judicial** dissolution in Tunis.

## 4. MOROCCO — Association (Dahir n° 1-58-376 du 15/11/1958, as modified by loi 75-00 via dahir n° 1-02-206 du 23/07/2002)
**Primary source:** [MenA Rights, dahir 1958 FR (PDF)](http://menarights.org/sites/default/files/2016-12/MOR_Associations_FR.pdf) (fetched, saved `/tmp/ma1958.txt`); [ICNL English translation](https://www.icnl.org/research/library/morocco_act-2002-en/); [SGG associations portal](https://www.sgg.gov.ma/Associations.aspx). Caveat: article numbers for the annual-report and dissolution clauses are not cleanly visible in the French extraction; ICNL's English lists arts. 20 and 23 — cited below as "dahir 1958 as modified by loi 75-00" with that caveat where applicable.

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Constitution | Declaration at prefecture (no prior authorisation) | Titre I |
| **Public-utility recognition** | By decree on application after prior administrative *enquête*; **motivated decision within max 6 months** of filing at the local authority; sports federations (art. 17 loi 06-87) recognised *de plein droit* by decree | Art. 9 (abrogé et remplacé, dahir 1-02-206 / loi 75-00) |
| **UP obligations (annual compliance)** | Faithful bookkeeping per regulation; *états de synthèse* + supporting pieces + books kept **5 years**; **annual report to the Secrétariat Général du Gouvernement** on the affectation of resources for each calendar year, **certified by an expert-comptable inscrit à l'ordre des experts comptables** attesting *sincérité des comptes* ("sous réserve des dispositions de la loi relative au code des juridictions financières"). **No CAC.** Recognition **withdrawable** after a warning to regularise accounting within **3 months**. UP associations get the Art. 6 privileges | Art. 9 block (same title) |
| Foreign aid | Must be declared to the SGG (amount + origin) **within 30 days** of receipt; infraction → **dissolution per Art. 7** | Art. 32 bis |
| **Judicial dissolution** | First-instance tribunal competent to hear **dissolution** of an association in a situation *non conforme à la loi*, at the request of **any concerned person** or on the initiative of the **ministère public**; tribunal may order (interim measures, text truncated). Foreign associations: nullity (Art. 3) or infraction of arts. 14, 23, 25 / ordre public → dissolution per Art. 7 procedure; founders/directors: 3–2 months' imprisonment | Art. 7 (as modified by dahir 1-02-206); foreign-association penalty clause |
| Spontaneous dissolution / asset devolution | **Art. 37 (as modified):** voluntary dissolution → assets per by-laws, à défaut per rules fixed by the **AG**; judicial dissolution → the **court** fixes liquidation modalities per by-laws or by derogation (special carve-out for UP associations, tail truncated) | Art. 37 |
| Penalties | Reconstituting/staying in being **illegally after judicial dissolution**: **1–6 months' imprisonment and/or 10,000–20,000 DH fine** (double on recidive); other infractions: 500–5,000 DH fines | Penal clauses (Titre I end, around Art. 32 quater) |

**Verdict: SETTLED with caveats.** Annual reporting + expert-comptable certification attach to the **utilité-publique regime** (and subsidy recipients), not to all associations; there is **no CAC** at any threshold. Dissolution is **judicial** (Art. 7), with a criminal penalty on post-dissolution reconstitution.

## 5. MAURITANIA — Association (Law n° 2021-004 du 10/02/2021) + foundation
**Primary source:** [MenA Rights, Law 2021-004 (PDF)](https://menarights.org/sites/default/files/2021-07/Law%20No.%202021-004%20of%2010%20February%202021%20on%20associations.pdf) (fetched, saved `/tmp/mt2021.txt`; law abrogates loi 64-098 du 09/06/1964, Art. 72).

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Constitution | Free constitution; **three-tier declaration**: national → Ministère de l'Intérieur, regional → Wali, local → Hakem; immediate signed *accusé*; **constitution by default if no decision within 60 days** | Arts. 5, 7, 9 |
| Bookkeeping | Faithful bookkeeping; états + supporting pieces + registre kept **≥ 5 years** | Arts. 17, 35 |
| **Audit (CAC)** | **No monetary threshold, but categorical:** **public-utility AND development associations must have a CAC** | **Art. 22** |
| **Annual compliance** | **Annual financial report to the Ministère des Finances AND the civil-society department, certified by the CAC** (attesting *sincérité des comptes*); **annual activity report** + synthesis **published "dans les médias nationaux"** (both attach to the UP/development category) | Arts. 36, 37 |
| **Suspension** | ≤ **30 days** by the Minister | Art. 23 |
| UP status withdrawal | After **mise en demeure to regularise within 3 months**: "Ce retrait n'est susceptible d'aucun recours" | Art. 38 |
| **Judicial dissolution** | Grounds: illegitimate purpose, *ordre public*, violation of law/by-laws, distribution of profits, out-of-scope activities; court seized by the **ministère public** or **two-thirds of the bureau** | Art. 25 |
| **Asset devolution** | Voluntary dissolution → per by-laws, à défaut per AG rules; judicial dissolution → per by-laws, sinon per legislation in force | **Art. 26** |
| Member challenge | One-third of members may challenge AG decisions via the procureur; administrative/judicial appeal against suspension | Arts. 29, 27–28 |
| Penalties | Absence of declaration (Art. 65); **reconstitution after dissolution (Art. 66)**; violation of information obligation (Art. 67); **subsidy misuse = *détournement de deniers publics*** (Art. 68) | Arts. 65–68 |
| Foundation | Min. own assets **10,000,000 MRU** per the law (**conflicts with the 3,000,000 MRU** in `enrich3-tasks.json` — flag); activities in ≥ 5 wilayas; constitutive act by notary | Arts. 45–46 |

**Verdict: SETTLED.** CAC and the certified annual reports attach to the **public-utility / development** category only (categorical, not monetary); dissolution is **judicial**; the non-appealable Art. 38 status withdrawal is the administrative exit route for UP associations.
