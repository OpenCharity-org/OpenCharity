# Legal-entity research: registered associations
**Israel · Jordan** — consolidated report, statutory text verified against primary sources. Emphasis: annual reporting obligations, filing deadlines, audit requirements, foreign-donor disclosure. All web content treated as untrusted; citations below.

## 1. ISRAEL — Amuta (association) under the Law of Associations 5740-1980 (in force 1981)

**Primary source:** full statute text via Wayback snapshot (2025-08-11) of nevo.co.il, `law_html/law00/4496.htm` (saved `/tmp/il_nevo_wb.txt`; direct nevo.co.il access is IP-blocked — do not retry direct).

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Financial report (dugach kesafi)** | Board prepares **once a year, for each tax year**: balance sheet + income/expense statement with full detail per Second Schedule; submitted to audit committee / external auditor **≥ 2 weeks before the general assembly**, then **tabled before the GA for approval** | **§36(א)** |
| **Top-5-salaries disclosure** | Must be **attached to the financial report**: full, precise detail of all payments paid or committed to each of the **top 5 highest-paid** recipients, including retirement-benefit conditions; "payment" includes anything of cash value, loans, securities | **§36(ב)** |
| **Filing deadline with the Registrar** | **June 30 of the year following the reporting period** ("לא יאוחר מיום 30 ביוני בשנה שלאחר תום תקופת הדו"ח"), original or duly certified copy, **signed by two board members**; **the Registrar may extend the deadline** | **§36(ד) [PINNED]** |
| **Verbal (textual) report (dugach miluli)** | Board prepares **once a year** a report on matters fixed by the Minister; **filed together with the financial report** and bound by §36(ד) → **same June 30 deadline** | **§37א(א)–(ג) [PINNED]** |
| **Online filing** | Both reports + the GA's approval protocol (with the report, the audit committee/auditor recommendation, and auditor's opinion if an auditor was appointed) must be filed **online**, signed by two board members | **§38(א)(3),(6),(6א); §38(ב)(3)** [PINNED: "דו"ח כספי ודוח מילולי יוגשו במועד הקבוע בסעיף 36(ד)"] |
| **Clarification duty** | Registrar may demand, within a set period, any document/info to clarify the financial report, and may demand an **amended report** | **§38א** |
| **Foreign-state-entity disclosure (2025)** | Association with turnover **> NIS 300,000** must state in the financial report whether it received donations from foreign state entities **> NIS 20,000** in aggregate; if yes: **donor identity, amount, purpose, conditions**; the report is **published on the association's website** (or MoJ site if the association has none) | **§36א(א)–(ג) [PINNED]** |
| **Audit regime** | No monetary audit threshold in the statute itself; audit-committee / external-auditor appointment is bylaws-driven (§36(א) addresses both). PBF (charitable-institution) rules add external-auditor requirements for larger entities — secondary source | §36(א); neo-project summary (secondary) |
| **Annual fee to the Registrar** | ≈ **NIS 1,500/year (2026)**; ≈ NIS 700 for below-low-turnover threshold (secondary; re-confirm with Registrar) | MoJ fee schedule (secondary) |
| **Tax (separate duty)** | Income report to the **Tax Authority ~90 days after fiscal-year end** — distinct from the Registrar filing; do not conflate | benorkok-cpa summary (secondary) |
| **Discrepancy note** | A secondary guide (hashavia.co.il) claims the Registrar deadline is **May 31** — **contradicts the statute**; the statute (June 30) is authoritative. The 30–90-day "ishur nihul takin" (valid activity certificate) window is a processing period, **not** a filing deadline | hashavia.co.il vs. §36(ד) |

**Verdict: SETTLED.** Two annual reports (financial + verbal) plus the GA-approval protocol are filed online with the Registrar of Associations by **June 30** following the reporting year; top-5-salaries and foreign-state-donor disclosures ride inside the financial report. The statute's own date (30 June) prevails over conflicting secondary guidance (31 May).

## 2. JORDAN — Private society (NGO) under Law No. 51 of 2008, as amended by Law No. 22 of 2009

**Primary source:** ICNL English translation of the full law, `Jordan_51-2008-En.pdf` (saved `/tmp/jo_law.txt`), from [ICNL library](https://www.icnl.org/research/library/jordan_51-2008-en); secondary: ICNL Civic Freedom Monitor 2025 (47pp, `/tmp/jo_cfm.txt`), two GPEI Jordan expert reports (2018, 2022).

| Requirement | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Annual package to the Ministry** | Board of directors must provide the **Relevant Ministry (MSD)** with: (1) **annual work plan**; (2) **annual report** — achievements, activities in the previous year, **sources of revenue and expenditure** — plus any statements required by the regulations; (3) **annual budget audited by a chartered accountant elected by the GA** | **Art. 16 [PINNED]** |
| **Audit threshold** | Minister **may exempt budgets below JOD 2,000** from the chartered-accountant audit; the ministry then **reviews the accounts itself** | **Art. 16** |
| **Donor disclosure in annual report** | Must declare **donations/funding received** with **donor identity, amount, purpose, and conditions** | **Art. 17 [PINNED]** |
| **Filing deadline** | **None in the law's text.** Art. 16 sets the duty, not the date. The natural home of any deadline is the **Regulation on Private Societies (MSD Regulation 32 of 2010)** — its text is **unreachable** (not published in English; no mirror found) and both GPEI expert reports are **negative** on a reg-level deadline. **GAP, flagged honestly** | ICNL CFM 2025; GPEI 2018/2022 (searched, negative) |
| **GA decisions to the ministry** | Decisions of the general assembly must be submitted to the ministry **within 15 days** | **Art. 14C1** |
| **Bylaw amendments** | Require **60-day** ministerial approval before taking effect | law, title III |
| **Tax (separate duty)** | Income Tax Law: return to the Income Tax Department **"no later than the last day of the fourth month following the end of the tax period, or upon request"** | GPEI 2022 (PINNED) |
| **Dissolution** | Voluntary (GA decision, court ratification) or judicial on enumerated grounds; assets per bylaws à défaut to a similar-purpose society | law, title V |

**Verdict: PARTIALLY SETTLED.** The annual obligation (work plan + annual report + donor disclosure + audited budget, with the JOD 2,000 exemption) is pinned from the statute; the **filing date is not in the statute** and the regulation that would set it (Reg 32 of 2010) is unreachable — the only firm calendar date is the tax return (end of 4th month after the tax period). Treat the annual-report deadline as **undetermined / practice-dependent on MSD practice** until the regulation is obtained.

## Cross-country comparison

| | Israel 5740-1980 | Jordan 51/2008 (+22/2009) |
|---|---|---|
| Regulator | Registrar of Associations (MoJ) | Ministry of Social Development |
| Annual reports to state | **2** (financial + verbal) + GA-approval protocol | **1 package** (annual report + work plan + statements) |
| **Filing deadline** | **June 30 following the reporting year** (statutory; Registrar may extend) | **None in statute**; Reg 32 of 2010 unreachable — **gap** |
| Pre-filing step | GA approval after audit-committee review ≥ 2 weeks pre-GA | GA ratification (bylaws-driven) |
| Donor disclosure | Foreign-state entities > NIS 20k (if turnover > NIS 300k), published on association site | **All donations**: identity, amount, purpose, conditions |
| Audit | Bylaws-driven; no monetary threshold in statute | Chartered accountant (GA-elected); **exempt < JOD 2,000** budget (ministry then reviews) |
| Other hard deadline | Top-5-salaries report (attached to financial report); tax report ~90 days post-FY | **GA decisions to ministry within 15 days**; tax return by end of 4th month post-period |
| Penalties for non-filing | Registrar clarification/amended-report power (§38א) | Law title IV (fines for violations; details in regulation) |

**Bottom line:** Israel gives a crisp, statutory, one-size-fits-all filing date — **June 30** — for both annual reports; Jordan pins the *content* of the annual package precisely (incl. a hard JOD 2,000 audit-exemption line) but leaves the *date* to a regulation that is not publicly obtainable. In both systems, donor-disclosure duties are statutory and travel inside the annual report, and the tax filing is a separate, independently dated obligation.
